# Figure S5C

Frozen primary all-disease associations and two negative-control analyses. The temporal placebo uses storm-season impact dates randomized at least 50 days from observed impacts (seed 20260207). The spatial placebo permutes exposure labels across ZIP codes independently within each date while preserving the date-specific exposure distribution (seed 20260224). All models include ZIP, ISO year-week, day-of-week, and ZIP-by-COVID-period fixed effects, with two-way clustering by ZIP and ISO year-week.
