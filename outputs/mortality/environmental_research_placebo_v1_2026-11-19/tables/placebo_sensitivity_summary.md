Notes: These are negative-control analyses using the same four age groups, four event-relative periods, fixed effects, and two-way clustering as the primary all-disease models. Temporal placebo: 11 of 16 confidence intervals exclude 1 and 16 of 16 point estimates share the primary direction; Spatial placebo: 0 of 16 confidence intervals exclude 1 and 8 of 16 point estimates share the primary direction. Placebo results are interpreted as falsification evidence, not as alternative estimates of a storm effect. No placebo-specific multiplicity adjustment was prespecified. The legacy temporal calendar keeps each randomized date at least 50 days from its corresponding observed storm, but not from every other observed storm: 4.83% of its active ZIP-days overlap an observed event window, and 581 ZIP-specific placebo events fall within 13 days of another observed impact. This partial overlap does not by itself explain the broadly positive temporal-placebo pattern, which warrants further temporal-control diagnostics.
