# Spatial figure captions

## Figure 5
Empirical-Bayes-smoothed ZIP-level rate ratios for the eight older-adult cause-period associations meeting the locked statewide spatial-eligibility rule. Color represents each ZIP's centered log EB rate ratio, displayed as a percentage deviation from the target-specific geometric mean. The same color limits are used across panels; gray indicates a ZIP without an estimable counterfactual expected count.

## Figure 6
Local Moran cluster classifications for the same eight older-adult targets using row-standardized queen contiguity and 9,999 permutations. Global p-values were Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted across the eight maps, and local p-values were adjusted across ZIPs within each map. No target met the global spatial-concentration criterion; 2 ZIPs had FDR-supported local classifications.

## Figure S8
Empirical-Bayes reliability and spatial-weight sensitivity. Panel A maps EB reliability, defined as expected deaths divided by the sum of the EB prior rate and expected deaths. Panel B compares global Moran's I under queen contiguity, rook contiguity, and symmetric six-nearest-neighbor weights. Spatial conclusions under all three definitions are reported in Table S9.
