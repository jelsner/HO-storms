# Figure S5. Alternative-specification analyses

Panel A compares the frozen primary all-disease estimates with a no-offset count model restricted to ZIP-years with positive annual population and with the corresponding population-offset model. Panel B excludes observations from 2020 through 2022. Panel C shows the legacy temporal and spatial placebo analyses. Panel D shows empirical null distributions from 199 strict temporal placebo calendars; orange lines are the frozen primary estimates and dashed lines are permutation medians. Panel E restricts cause-specific models to the ICD-10 era (1999-2022). Panel F excludes the 5,912 ZIP-dates appearing in more than one event window before closest-impact assignment. Points and bars in panels A-C, E, and F are rate ratios and 95% two-way cluster-robust confidence intervals. Complete estimates are reported in Table S8.
