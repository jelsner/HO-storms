## Agreement across all 80 estimands

The population-offset estimates agreed in direction with the frozen primary and covered-sample count estimates for 80/80 estimands. All 80 offset confidence intervals overlapped both comparators.

The median and maximum absolute offset-versus-covered-sample differences on the log-RR scale were 0.000180 and 0.000364, respectively. The population-covered calendar retained 94.36% of primary ZIP-days.

Age-stratified offset models use total annual ZIP population because age-specific ZIP denominators are unavailable; they are interpreted as population-scale adjustments rather than age-specific mortality-rate models.
