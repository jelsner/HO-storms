# Main figure captions

## Figure 1
Study design and event-relative periods. Florida ZIP-day mortality from 1985 through 2022 was aligned to ZIP-specific tropical-cyclone impact dates defined at sustained winds of at least 34 kt. Four seven-day periods preserved event-relative timing; all retained non-event ZIP-days formed the reference.

## Figure 2
All-disease mortality relative to ZIP-specific impact. Points are fixed-effects Poisson rate ratios and horizontal lines are 95% two-way cluster-robust confidence intervals. Filled points satisfy the Benjamini-Hochberg q<0.05 criterion across the 16 primary tests.

## Figure 3
All-age cause-specific mortality across the four event-relative periods. Color distinguishes the prioritized secondary and exploratory families. Filled points satisfy the conservative Benjamini-Hochberg q<0.05 criterion across all 160 cause-specific tests.

## Figure 4
Age-specific associations for the four prioritized causes. Points and confidence intervals are from separate age-specific models; displayed q-values are from global two-degree-of-freedom age-heterogeneity tests in jointly fitted stacked-age models with FDR correction across 40 cause-period tests.
