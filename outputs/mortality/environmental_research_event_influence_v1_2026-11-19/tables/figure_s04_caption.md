# Figure S4

Leave-one-impact-date-out ranges for the 16 all-disease age-period estimates. Each blue point represents an exact point-estimate refit after omitting all ZIP-day event-window observations assigned to one of 77 distinct impact dates; blue line segments show the resulting range. Diamonds and thick gray bars show the frozen full-sample estimate and 95% confidence interval, respectively. No jackknife confidence intervals were estimated.
